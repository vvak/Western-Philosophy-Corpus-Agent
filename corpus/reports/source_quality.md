## Planned Checks

- Exact duplicate hash
- Paragraph-level near-duplicate detection

## Initial Quality Policy

Prefer curated ebook sources before OCR:

1. Standard Ebooks
2. Project Gutenberg
3. Perseus or similar structured classical text repositories with clear license
4. Internet Archive and HathiTrust OCR after quality gates exist
